Tomb Candles

Three documented 4×4 candle arrangements use empty, unlit, and lit states. Their purpose remains unknown.

Unsolved

What We Know

Setup: Three candle arrangements have been documented on 4×4 stands in the underground tombs. Across the complete stands, candles occupy six coordinates: A1, C1, C2, B3, A4, and D4. One arrangement retains only unlit candles at C1 and D4, another has all six candles with three lit, and the third has all six lit.

The 4×4 format recurs: The same grid dimensions appear in the boiler room panels (×15), the boiler room elevator top, the attic box, the garage gas tank, the darkroom lower cabinet, the conservatory box, the pool dig spot, the tunnel to the sanctum doors, underpass hallway walls, throne room pillar bases, and more. Shared dimensions are confirmed; shared data or encoding rules are not.

Three observable states: Each cell can be recorded as empty, occupied by an unlit candle, or occupied by a lit candle. This supports a ternary transcription, but it does not establish that the game treats the states as numeric values.

No confirmed input found: No 4×4 input mechanism has been identified. The 2×8 trophy import screen in Blackbridge Grotto is another 16-cell interface, but no connection to the candles has been demonstrated.

Alzara connection: The candles appear in one of Alzara's visions, but they cannot be interacted with during the vision sequence.

Three arrangements have been documented. One contains two unlit candles, one contains six candles with three lit, and one contains the same six candle positions with all six lit. Every cell can therefore be transcribed as empty, unlit, or lit.

Theories (Ranked)

Promising

Connection to Boiler Room panels

The Boiler Room has 15 panels with the same 4×4 dimensions, while the Tomb adds a third observable state. A comparison under justified orientations and state mappings may reveal a relationship, but no shared symbol or positional mapping has been demonstrated.

Speculative

Stacking grids to produce letter values

The three states can be assigned values and overlaid, but no evidence identifies which grids to combine, how to orient or weight them, or how the resulting sums should map to letters. The disproved universal top-left constraint also removes one proposed anchor.

Speculative

Binary encoding: each row = 4 bits (0-15)

Reading each row as four bits would produce values from 0-15, but a binary conversion must collapse either empty and unlit or unlit and lit into the same value. No evidence identifies the intended collapse, reading direction, or output format.

Speculative

Route cipher or Polybius square

A 4×4 layout can support route ciphers or a modified Polybius square, but no route, alphabet, key, or source text has been tied to these candle positions. Fitting a 16-character phrase to 16 cells is not independent evidence.

Speculative

Four grids combine into an 8×8 chessboard

Four 4×4 grids can form an 8×8 board, but only three Tomb arrangements are documented here and no fourth grid, quadrant order, or chess-piece mapping has been identified.

Speculative

Underpass hallway arch rivets encode 4/8-bit data

The Underpass arches have rivets that can be grouped visually into sets of four or eight, and nearby wall panels use 4×4 filled and empty squares. No bit order, repeated value, or correspondence to the Tomb grids has been demonstrated.

Speculative

Freezer server lights as related encoding

The Freezer server lights form on/off patterns that resemble other estate grids. They do not align cleanly with Braille, and no orientation or mapping connects them to the Tomb candles.

Speculative

Trophy import as potential 16-bit input

The Blackbridge Grotto trophy import screen exposes 16 selectable positions in a 2×8 layout. No trigger, ordering rule, or evidence connects those selections to the 4×4 candle data.

Speculative

Dirigiblocks update will provide the key

The candle grids may be intentionally unsolvable in the current version. The Dirigiblocks update could introduce new rooms, mechanics, or input methods that give these patterns meaning. Several cut-content elements may be restored.

Speculative

Decorative red herring with thematic purpose

The repeated 4×4 format may come from modular environmental design rather than a shared cipher. The distinct Tomb states still make the candle arrangements worth documenting, but repetition alone does not prove encoded content.
