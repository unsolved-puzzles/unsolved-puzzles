The Eye Puzzle

9 messages made of eye glyphs found in parallel worlds. Not an in-game interaction: Ghidra decompilation confirmed no trigger exists. This is a pure cryptography puzzle. The cipher is polyalphabetic with 83 internal states. Devs confirmed the messages contain real information.
