Castle, Court, Cloak

Three words on a screen. Binary spells ARIE. 140+ hours later, nobody knows why.

Unsolved

What We Know

Setup: After finding and inserting 3 microchips in the Blackbridge Grotto computer, a cutscene plays showing three words in sequence: Castle, Court, Cloak. Between the words, lines and dots appear that can be interpreted as binary.

Binary decode: The binary between words translates to: 01000001 01010010 01001001 01000101 = A R I E. Each letter lasts about a quarter second and repeats on loop.

Timing anomaly: When the words scroll fast, it reads "clock" not "cloak". The word "cloak" only appears when the animation slows down. This may be deliberate misdirection or a rendering artifact.

The mystery: What do these three words mean together? Is "ARIE" a truncation of "ARIES"? Is this foreshadowing, a hint, or encoded instructions?

Theories (Ranked)

High Priority

Key of Aries hint: Castle + Court + Cloak = three steps to the throne

Castle puzzle leads to the Court of Aries, where the clock (cloak) hides the Key. The binary "ARIE" confirms the Aries connection. This may be a compressed walkthrough for reclaiming the throne, with "cloak" as double meaning (the clock + the prince's ceremonial cloak).

High Priority

Missing letter pattern: ARIE needs one more letter

ARIE is clearly ARIES minus one letter (S). The game loves missing letters (keyboards missing I, Herbert's missing letter #3). Perhaps the three words + ARIE are a puzzle themselves: find the missing letter/word to complete the sequence.

Medium

Clock/Cloak deliberate ambiguity encodes two paths

At fast speed = "clock" (literal clock puzzle in the tomb). At slow speed = "cloak" (concealment, the hidden nature of the ruins). Both readings are valid simultaneously, typical of the game's layered design.

Medium

ARG component: community must collaborate

Major Key wrote that "those who try to solve a puzzle alone will only find rage." This puzzle might require outside-the-game collaboration, similar to Binding of Isaac's Keeper unlock. The data might be intentionally incomplete in-game.

Lower Priority

Synka Court office connection

One of Synka's offices is called "Synka Court." The word "court" might reference the corporate structure rather than (or in addition to) the Court of Aries. Less supported but worth noting.
