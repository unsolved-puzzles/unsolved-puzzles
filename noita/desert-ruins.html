The Desert Ruins Markings

Procedural desert ruins bear three ovals, a triangle sequence, and a diamond. Hints for the eyes, or ancient lore?

Debated

What We Know

Ruins generate randomly across the surface of the desert, and some are carved with small, deliberate-looking markings.

A handful of distinct motifs recur across these ruins. Like the Master Statues, they read as more purposeful than typical background decoration, which is why the community scrutinises them.

Several of the motifs echo symbols seen elsewhere in Noita, from alchemical signs to the game's recurring three-eye imagery.

Confirmed

The three eyes (three ovals)

A set of three ovals arranged in a triangle. It echoes Noita's pervasive three-eye motif (the boss Kolmisilma = 'three-eye', the three creation eggs, the Master of Masters' robe) and also resembles the alchemical symbol for oil.

Source: FuryForged
Confirmed

The rotated square (diamond)

A diamond glyph. Diamonds recur in-game: the essence chambers were formerly diamond-shaped, and the diamond is also the alchemical symbol for soap and echoes the spell-refresh icon.

Source: FuryForged
Confirmed

The three inverted 'i's (down/up/down)

A row of three triangular strokes in alternating orientation, mirroring the alternating up/down grouping used to read the eye trigrams.

Source: FuryForged
Tentative

More common in the East parallel world

Ruins bearing these markings appear more often in the East parallel world (several, each with diamonds) and don't show up on the in-game map.

Source: u/-Fatmeatball-

Theories (Ranked)

Promising

Reading-order hint for the eyes

The down/up/down triangle sequence plus the diamond may encode how to read (and rotate) the eye trigrams.

Speculative

Just lore

The markings may be decorative lore handed down by the earlier civilization, with no puzzle content. The diamond could be the essence chambers, spell refresh, or even the alchemic symbol for soap.

Speculative

The diamond is the spell-refresh icon (or Verikristalli)

A common reading is that the diamond is simply the spell-refresh symbol; an alternative is the Verikristalli (blood crystal), which is also diamond-shaped. Source: u/Complete-Basket-291

Speculative

The strokes encode directions

The three strokes may be movement instructions (up, down, up with distances) rather than an eye reading-order hint. Source: u/Complete-Basket-291
