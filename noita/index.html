Open Cases

The Eye Puzzle

9 messages made of eye glyphs found in parallel worlds. Not an in-game interaction: Ghidra decompilation confirmed no trigger exists. This is a pure cryptography puzzle. The cipher uses a polyalphabetic mechanism with hidden internal states far exceeding 83. Devs confirmed the messages contain real information.

The Cauldron Room

A hidden room behind Extremely Dense Rock with a mysterious cauldron, Void Liquid calendar, and connections to the Cessation Cipher. 5+ years of experiments, still no answer.

Unsolved

The Alchemy Symbol

A dollar-sign-in-a-capsule buried above the High Alchemist's Arena. Attributed to Petri, like the eyes.

Unsolved

The Master Statues

Three spell-master statues marked P, B, G over distinct shapes, plus a defaced fourth. A puzzle, or lore disguised as decoration?

Unsolved

Music-Attracted Animals

Only innocent creatures swarm the music machines and disperse when the music stops. Why program this?

The Desert Ruins Markings
