The Master Statues

Three spell-master statues marked P, B, G over distinct shapes, plus a defaced fourth. A puzzle, or lore disguised as decoration?

Unsolved

What We Know

In the Temple of the Art stand three statues of spell-casting masters: teleportation, blinding, and polymorphing.

Each is marked with a glyph over a distinct shape and holds an orb at a different height. Right-to-left the glyphs are P, B, G.

There is also a fourth statue, defaced, with only the bottom third remaining.

Confirmed

Three masters, glyphs P/B/G

Teleportation, blinding, polymorphing masters; each glyph sits over a different shape; each holds an orb at a different position.

Source: FuryForged
Confirmed

A fourth statue is defaced

Only the bottom third of the fourth statue remains.

Source: FuryForged
Tentative

P = Polymorph, B = Blinding

Two of the three glyphs line up with their mage. Teleportation = G is unexplained.

Source: FuryForged
Confirmed

The sarcophagi were once enemies

The common Temple-of-the-Art sarcophagi used to spawn a ghoul when approached; their script broke, so the devs kept them as decoration (per a dev message on the Noita Discord).

Source: u/Samug, dev
Tentative

Rare red-eye, three-eyed sarcophagus variant

A scarce variant (only ~2 found) has three glowing red eyes and glyphs unlike the known legend; its rarity suggests significance and it may tie to the game's three-eye motif.

Source: u/ShwaMallah, u/Deuphoric
Tentative

Undeciphered glyphs (a possible third glyph set)

These statues and nearby background doors bear glyphs that match neither of Noita's two known glyph languages.

Source: u/ShwaMallah

Theories (Ranked)

Promising

Glyphs map to the objects, not the mages

The P/B/G glyphs may correspond to the shapes/objects (egg, vase, mountain?) each statue holds, not to the mage type.

Promising

The glyphs depict specific wands

Several solvers note the unmatched glyphs resemble unique wand sprites, hinting you may need to bring or use particular wands at the statues. Source: u/Rantar508, u/Nombre_D_Usuario

Speculative

Orb + death-count interaction

Kick or carry an Orb to the red-eye statues, possibly at a specific death count (the night-sky star count equals deaths). One thread even aligns the statue's orb and stars with sky structures as a route to a 'natural' 34th orb. Source: u/Winged_Blade, u/ShwaMallah

Speculative

The fourth master was technology/grounding

The defaced statue may be a master of technology (grounding), struck from the record by mages who saw technology as a perversion of magic.

Speculative

Important lore disguised as decoration

It may not be an interactive puzzle at all, but lore the order of masters passed down.
